This function implements the training loop of the Recurrent Neural Network. It automatically performs the PyTorch conversion internally, so both its inputs and its outputs are in Numpy format. Each iteration consist of a simulation, a loss computation and a backpropagation. After a certain amount of iterations, a simulation is carried on the whole dataset to evaluate the performance and report it together with plots. The final outputs of this function are the improvement matrices best_A_prime and best_B_prime, i.e. the correction masks to sum to the model.
